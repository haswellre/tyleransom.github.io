About Me

I am a PhD student in Statistics at Qufu Normal University , China. I hold a Master's degree in Regional Economics from Yangzhou University . My academic interests lie at the intersection of applied statistics, regional economics, environmental economics, and public policy.

My research focuses on the economic and spatial implications of water resource constraints, particularly their effects on agricultural production, industrial transformation, and regional development. I am interested in understanding how environmental regulations, resource allocation, and regional policies influence economic behavior and spatial outcomes.

Methodologically, I am particularly interested in applied econometrics, spatial econometrics, causal inference, and quantitative approaches to regional and environmental analysis. My research emphasizes the integration of economic mechanisms, empirical identification, and spatial analysis in evaluating policy interventions.

Research Interests

  • Water Resource Economics
    Water resource constraints, agricultural production, water footprints, and sustainable resource allocation.
  • Regional and Environmental Economics
    Regional development, industrial transformation, environmental regulation, and spatial economic outcomes.
  • Policy Evaluation and Causal Inference
    Environmental and regional policy evaluation using quasi-experimental methods and panel data.
  • Spatial Econometrics
    Spatial dependence, spatial spillovers, and geographical heterogeneity in socioeconomic processes.
  • Watershed Governance
    Transboundary water governance, environmental management, and intergovernmental coordination.

Research in Progress

Water Footprints and Food Production Resilience in China
Work in Progress

This project investigates the relationship between agricultural water footprints and food production resilience in China, with particular attention to spatial dependence, regional heterogeneity, and policy implications.

Spatial Restructuring of Water Environmental Control Units and Transboundary Pollution Governance
Work in Progress

This research examines how the spatial organization of water environmental control units influences pollution responsibility allocation and intergovernmental coordination across administrative boundaries.
